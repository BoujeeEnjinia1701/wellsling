# WellSling STEM check

Verdict: the working principle of WellSling, lifting a 600 kg animal out of a 15 m well with a frame, a braked hand winch and two webbing bands, all worked from the rim, sits within known physics, chemistry and biology. Of 48 claims, 37 are within known science and 11 are plausible, to confirm at TRL 4. No row fails on the checks made, but the dynamic load of a kicking animal, the soil and lining at real wells and the animal's tolerance of the bands are open, not proven. The check found seven details that failed. Each was fixed in the same session under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23 and closed: the winch leg missed its factor of 3 once an initial bow is counted, four joints of the kit 1.7 design could not be assembled, option B's beam pieces were too heavy to carry, and (found by the independent review) the 1.5 times proof load exceeded the winch's working rating on its third layer. Eight of the plausible items belong to the chosen design (S6, S14, S15, S25, S26, S34, S36, S39) and need a winch datasheet, a site, a veterinarian or a dummy trial, so `stem_check` is **open**. Method and rules: STANDARDS section 20. Calculations: `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py`, which reads the results of WSL-CAL-001 (`docs/04-calcs/sizing.py`) and the parametric model.

> **Safety:** WellSling lifts a heavy, frightened animal over an open well. Nothing in this check replaces a proof-load test of the real tripod, winch, rope and rigging at 1.5 times the hook load, which is TRL 4 work. Nobody goes down the well at any stage.

## Fields not relevant

All five fields are touched: physics (statics, strength of materials, thermodynamics of the lift), chemistry (zinc coating and polyester webbing), biology (the bad air the kit avoids, the animal in the bands, human effort and carrying), mathematics (energy, time, heights and reach) and engineering and technology (rope practice, joints that must assemble, setup time, control of a draught haul). ## Concept options

- **Option A, tripod and hand winch (the design since kit 1.7):** within known science. Eight rows are plausible and open for TRL 4 (S6 winch handle force, S14 soil bearing, S15 rim and lining, S25 animal tolerance of the bands, S26 passer push force, S34 setup time, S36 dynamic load of a kicking animal, S39 breaking free of mud). Six failed details are fixed (S12, S31, S32, S33, S35, S38).
- **Option B, portable gantry and two chain hoists:** within known science. The beam and A-frames have factors of 3.1 and 39 (B1, B2). The hand chain pull is plausible (B3). The beam was resectioned to meet the carry limit (B4).
- **Option C, sheerlegs and draught haul:** within known science for the frame (C5, factor 7.6 on Euler and 12.0 on yield with an initial bow), the tractor pull (C2) and the snubbing post (C3, about 400 N to hold with three turns). The guy anchor's holding in soft ground (C1) and smooth control of a tractor haul (C4) are plausible only and carry the option's main risk.

No option has a **Fails** row, so none is dropped.
